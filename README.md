# Awesome-Corporate-Performance-Management

## Top Corporate Performance Management Tools Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS/Hosted Platforms & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Corporate Performance Management, Enterprise Planning, FP&A, Budgeting, Forecasting, Financial Consolidation & Management Reporting*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Corporate Performance Management (CPM)**. These tools help organizations plan, budget, forecast, consolidate financial results, analyze performance, manage strategic plans, model scenarios, and provide management reporting across finance and operational functions.



**Examples** include OneStream, Anaplan, Oracle EPM, SAP Analytics Cloud, Planful, CCH Tagetik, Board, Jedox, Prophix, Workiva, and other enterprise performance-management platforms. These products span financial planning and analysis, enterprise planning, consolidation, reporting, profitability management, and connected planning.



Corporate Performance Management overlaps with **Enterprise Performance Management (EPM), Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), Extended Planning & Analysis (xP&A), budgeting, forecasting, financial consolidation, management reporting, strategic planning, profitability management, scenario planning, workforce planning, operational planning, and business intelligence**.



**Open-source emphasis**: This repository heavily emphasizes open-source software that can be self-hosted and adapted into custom CPM/FP&A environments. Unlike areas such as BI or ERP, there are relatively few mature open-source projects that provide the *full* breadth of enterprise CPM suites. Therefore, the open-source section combines dedicated EPM projects with open-source ERP/financial-planning systems and the analytics, OLAP, data-warehouse, workflow, and forecasting components commonly used to build CPM platforms.



A particularly notable emerging project is **Konsolidat**, which describes itself as an MIT-licensed open-source EPM platform focused on consolidation, allocations, budgeting, variance analysis, and Excel-native workflows.



The goal is to provide a practical ecosystem map ranging from **enterprise CPM/EPM suites** to **developer-oriented open-source building blocks for creating custom corporate-performance platforms**.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or GitHub repositories.

### Recommended Open-Source Combinations



**Lightweight FP&A Stack**



`ERPNext + PostgreSQL + dbt + Metabase`



Suitable for smaller organizations that need accounting, budgeting, financial reporting, and management dashboards without a large commercial EPM deployment.



**Open-Source EPM Stack**



`Konsolidat + ClickHouse + dbt + Excel + Metabase`



A particularly interesting architecture for organizations seeking a dedicated open-source EPM layer around consolidation, budgeting, allocations, and variance analysis. Konsolidat explicitly targets these enterprise-finance use cases.



**Forecasting-Heavy FP&A Stack**



`ERPNext/Odoo + dbt + ClickHouse + StatsForecast + MLForecast + Superset`



Useful for automated rolling forecasts, revenue projections, expense forecasts, workforce planning, and scenario analysis.



**Data-First CPM Stack**



`Airbyte → Kafka → ClickHouse → dbt → Forecasting Models → Superset`



Suitable for organizations that already have ERP and operational systems but want to build a centralized planning and performance analytics layer.



**Enterprise Open-Source CPM Architecture**



`ERP/CRM/HR → Airbyte/NiFi → Kafka → ClickHouse → dbt → Konsolidat/Custom EPM → Forecasting → Superset/Metabase`



This architecture separates transactional systems from the analytical and planning layers, allowing organizations to progressively build CPM functionality rather than replacing the entire finance stack.



**AI-Enhanced CPM Stack**



`ERP Data → dbt → ClickHouse → Forecasting Models → LLM/AI Layer → Scenario Engine → Management Dashboards`



AI can be added for variance explanations, management-report generation, anomaly detection, driver identification, forecasting assistance, and natural-language querying of financial models.



## Important Corporate Performance Management Concepts



A complete CPM system typically combines several capabilities:



* **Financial Planning** — Planning revenue, expenses, margins, capital, and other financial outcomes.

* **FP&A** — Financial planning and analysis for management decision-making.

* **Budgeting** — Creating annual, quarterly, monthly, and departmental budgets.

* **Rolling Forecasting** — Continuously updating forecasts as new actuals become available.

* **Driver-Based Planning** — Building plans from operational drivers rather than purely financial line items.

* **Scenario Planning** — Comparing base, upside, downside, and alternative business scenarios.

* **What-If Analysis** — Modeling the financial effect of hypothetical decisions.

* **Sensitivity Analysis** — Measuring how changes in assumptions affect outcomes.

* **Financial Consolidation** — Combining financial results across entities, subsidiaries, and business units.

* **Intercompany Elimination** — Eliminating intercompany transactions during consolidation.

* **Foreign Currency Translation** — Translating subsidiary results into reporting currencies.

* **Minority Interest / NCI** — Accounting for non-controlling interests.

* **Account Reconciliation** — Reconciling balances and financial accounts.

* **Management Reporting** — Producing executive and management reports.

* **Financial Reporting** — Producing structured financial statements and reports.

* **Variance Analysis** — Comparing actuals against budgets, forecasts, and prior periods.

* **Profitability Analysis** — Understanding profitability by product, customer, region, channel, or business unit.

* **Cost Allocation** — Allocating shared costs across departments, products, or entities.

* **Workforce Planning** — Modeling headcount, compensation, hiring, attrition, and labor costs.

* **Sales Planning** — Forecasting pipeline, bookings, revenue, quotas, and sales capacity.

* **Supply-Chain Planning** — Connecting operational plans to financial outcomes.

* **Capital Planning** — Planning investments, capital expenditures, and asset requirements.

* **Cash-Flow Planning** — Forecasting cash inflows, outflows, liquidity, and working capital.

* **Strategic Planning** — Translating corporate strategy into measurable financial and operational targets.

* **KPI Management** — Monitoring business and financial performance indicators.

* **Driver Trees** — Connecting operational drivers to financial outcomes.

* **Long-Range Planning** — Modeling multi-year strategic plans.

* **Bottom-Up Planning** — Collecting plans from departments, business units, and operating teams.

* **Top-Down Planning** — Translating executive targets into organizational plans.

* **Connected Planning** — Linking financial and operational planning across departments.

* **xP&A** — Extending FP&A principles beyond Finance into workforce, sales, supply chain, and other functions.

* **Data Governance** — Maintaining consistent definitions, hierarchies, dimensions, and financial data.

* **Auditability** — Maintaining an auditable history of planning, adjustments, approvals, and reporting.

* **Workflow & Approvals** — Managing budget submissions, reviews, approvals, and close processes.

* **Version Management** — Maintaining multiple budget, forecast, and scenario versions.

* **Master Data Management** — Maintaining consistent accounts, entities, cost centers, products, and organizational hierarchies.

* **Narrative Reporting** — Connecting quantitative results to written management commentary.

* **Management Dashboards** — Providing executives with real-time or periodic views of business performance.

* **Embedded Analytics** — Bringing analytics directly into planning and finance workflows.

* **Predictive Forecasting** — Using statistical and machine-learning models to generate forecasts.

* **AI-Assisted Planning** — Using AI for forecasting, anomaly detection, variance explanations, and scenario generation.

* **Continuous Planning** — Moving beyond annual budgeting toward continuously updated forecasts and plans.

* **Financial Close Management** — Coordinating activities required to close accounting periods.

* **ESG / Sustainability Planning** — Extending performance management to environmental and sustainability metrics.

* **Regulatory Reporting** — Producing reports required by regulators and governing bodies.



## How to Contribute



1. Fork the repo.

2. Add/edit entries in `README.md` (follow the existing format).

3. Include: name, official link or GitHub repository, 1–2 sentence description, and whether it is SaaS/hosted or open-source.

4. Prefer projects with active repositories and meaningful documentation.

5. For open-source projects, accurately identify the primary capability — EPM, budgeting, forecasting, ERP/financial management, BI, consolidation, analytics infrastructure, or supporting infrastructure.

6. Do not label an ERP, BI tool, or forecasting library as a full CPM platform unless it actually provides CPM functionality.

7. Verify the project's current license before adding it.

8. Submit a PR with a short explanation.



Star the repo if you find it useful!



## Disclaimer



* This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

* Corporate Performance Management overlaps significantly with EPM, FP&A, ERP, BI, financial consolidation, planning, forecasting, and management reporting.

* The open-source ecosystem for **full enterprise CPM/EPM suites is substantially smaller than the commercial ecosystem**. Many open-source projects therefore provide individual layers rather than a complete OneStream/Anaplan/Oracle EPM equivalent.

* Open-source, source-available, open-core, and hosted offerings are not equivalent. Always verify the current license and self-hosting terms before adoption.

* Some commercial platforms provide substantially broader consolidation, governance, workflow, audit, and enterprise-planning capabilities than individual open-source components.

* Financial planning and reporting systems should be validated against applicable accounting standards, internal controls, audit requirements, and regulatory obligations.

* Forecasts are inherently uncertain and should not be treated as guarantees of future financial performance.

* Production CPM systems should implement appropriate access controls, audit trails, data governance, segregation of duties, backup, disaster recovery, and security controls.
